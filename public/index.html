I believe in three things.

01

People are at the center of any change

Even the best strategy or solution will struggle if people don't understand it, see its value, or know how to use it.

02

We can't change what we don't understand

Impactful change starts with creating a shared understanding of the challenge, the context, and the direction we're heading.

03

The best solution isn't the perfect one, it's the one that works

Rather than having the most ideal solution, progress comes from practical solutions that can be implemented, tested, learned from, and improved.

Whether it's strategy consulting, change management, business development, or communications — I believe successful execution starts and ends with people.

Developing a new strategy in a complex organizational situation, where creating understanding and commitment across the organization was essential for successful implementation.

Supporting Product Owners in developing their strategic capabilities and applying strategy in their everyday work.
